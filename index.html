Questions

Before you write to us

Another question? Ask us directly on WhatsApp.

Ask on WhatsApp
How long does publication take?

It depends on each media outlet. Your quote gives the timeline of each one, before you commit.

How much does it cost?

Each media outlet sets its own price. You get a clear quote per country and per outlet, and you choose only what fits your budget.

Do you write the articles?

Yes, if you want us to. We can write your article, edit your draft or translate it into the local language.

Can you publish in a country that is not listed?

Yes. Send us the country, and we confirm the available media and their prices before you commit.

Who do you work with?

Mainly crypto, fintech, payments and mining companies. We do not work with anonymous projects or with companies under sanctions.

Can my agency resell your publications?

Yes. Consultants, agencies and freelancers can offer our publications to their clients and keep their margin. Ask us for the partner price list.
